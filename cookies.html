A minimal approach

This version of the website does not use advertising cookies, analytics pixels or third-party embedded media.

Essential browser storage

The key avero-privacy-notice stores your acknowledgement of the compact privacy notice. It does not identify you across websites and is not used for advertising.

Form security

On a PHP host, the form may use a short-lived server session and an anti-forgery token to protect enquiry submissions. These are used only for security.

Your controls

You can remove stored website data using your browser settings. Clearing it will make the privacy notice appear again. You can continue browsing without acknowledging the notice.

Future changes

If optional analytics or advertising technologies are introduced, this policy and the consent controls must be updated before those technologies are activated.

Essential form session

On PHP hosting, the contact form may create a session cookie to validate requests and protect against automated submissions.

Local acknowledgement

The privacy notice acknowledgement is saved in localStorage on your device until you clear it.

Advertising technologies

This version does not install Google Ads, Meta Pixel or audience remarketing tags.

Browser controls

You can clear saved site data through your browser settings. This may reset the notice and an active form session.
